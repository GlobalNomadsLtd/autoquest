Cookie Policy for AutoQuest App

Effective Date: 8th April 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AutoQuest App ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you use our mobile application and any associated services.

1. What Are Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you interact with an app or website. In a mobile app environment, we may also use similar technologies such as software development kits (SDKs), local storage, device identifiers (like Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID), and tracking pixels. These technologies help the app function, remember your preferences, and provide us with analytics.

2. How We Use These Technologies

AutoQuest App utilizes tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Strictly Necessary / Essential: These are required for the app to function properly. They enable the local storage of data required for core features, such as calculating location-based news, caching on-the-day events (distance from user), and securely saving your photos to your device. The app cannot function properly without them.
  • Performance and Analytics: These help us understand how users interact with the app. They collect information about which features are used most often and any errors encountered, allowing us to improve the app's performance.
  • Functionality: These allow the app to remember choices you make (such as your login details, distance unit preferences like Miles vs. Kilometres, your subscription status, and your previously granted device permissions) to provide a more personalized experience without asking you every time.
  • Targeting and Advertising (If Applicable): These are used to deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests. They may also be used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

3. Third-Party Technologies

We may partner with third-party service providers who use SDKs or similar technologies within AutoQuest App. These third parties may collect information about your online activities over time and across different applications.

Specific Third-Party Tools We Use:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve native banner and rewarded video advertisements to free users. AdMob utilizes your device's Advertising ID to provide relevant ads and measure performance.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage): Used for user account creation, logging in, and securely backing up "Spotted" vehicles to the cloud.
  • Geolocation Services (Geolocator/Geocoding): Used to cache and calculate your distance from automotive events and display relevant location data.

4. How to Manage Your Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential tracking technologies. You can manage your preferences through the following methods:

  • In-App Settings: You can adjust a selection of preferences directly within the AutoQuest App settings page.
  • Device Settings (iOS): You can limit ad tracking by going to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and toggling off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" for AutoQuest App.
  • Device Settings (Android): You can manage your advertising ID by going to Settings > Privacy > Ads, where you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy or through an in-app notification.
